About us

We know each other from practice.

OrgVolution was founded by people who had already worked together. Over the years we got to know each other at different companies, tackled challenges together and saw how the others tick. We know what we have in each other and what we can rely on the others for.

At some point the question came up: why don't we work together more often? That's how OrgVolution grew, step by step. We brought people into the team whom we enjoy working with and whose work we believe in.

OrgVolution is owned by us. We're not building a company that has to become as big as possible. We want to do the work we're good at and enjoy, with people we value. And we want to help organisations genuinely make things better.
